# RAVINE Foundation Charter

## Purpose

RAVINE exists to expand human understanding and connection through creativity, culture, knowledge, and meaningful expression.

## Foundational commitments

1. Human dignity comes before commercial optimization.
2. Freedom of expression is protected alongside transparent, proportionate safeguards against abuse.
3. Popularity, reach, and engagement are not substitutes for quality, truth, or positive impact.
4. The platform does not claim ownership of truth; it makes room for evidence, belief, interpretation, opinion, hypothesis, and lived experience to be distinguished.
5. Creators retain their rights. Uploading a work does not itself prove ownership or grant the platform unlimited rights.
6. Economic systems should reward meaningful contribution fairly and transparently.
7. Platform power must be explainable, reviewable, and accountable.
8. Technology serves people; people do not exist to feed engagement systems.

## Scope

RAVINE is broader than cinema while retaining a cinematic sensibility. It can include film, video, audio, writing, books, research, lectures, photography, visual art, music, dialogue, cultural heritage, documentation, and archives.

## Identity

RAVINE is intended as a global human platform with Palestinian, Arab, and Islamic roots. Global openness does not require erasing those roots.

## Governance of decisions

This charter records direction, not permission to silently finalize unresolved product, policy, legal, or commercial choices. Such choices remain listed in `OPEN_DECISIONS.md` until explicitly approved.
