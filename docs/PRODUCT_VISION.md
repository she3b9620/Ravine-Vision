# RAVINE Product Vision

## Product shape

RAVINE combines a cinematic discovery and viewing experience with a human-centered social and knowledge network. The audience experience is a primary product surface from the beginning; the professional Creator Space is a distinct workspace rather than the default experience for everyone.

## Three connected universes

- **Personal Universe:** a person's account, relationships, interests, and activity.
- **Creator Universe:** one or more public creative identities and their professional presence.
- **Work / Expression Universe:** the works, projects, collaborations, collections, and experiences people publish or preserve.

A personal account may eventually manage multiple creator identities. This is a product direction; detailed permissions and workflows remain OPEN.

## Discovery scope

RAVINE is broader than cinema. It should support audiovisual works, podcasts, documentaries, films, series, photography, visual art, music, writing, books, research, lectures, conversations, cultural heritage, and archives.

## Creator Space

The professional space should support work management, projects, collaborators, audience relationships, community, rights information, performance context, and earnings—introduced in phases with clear permissions.

## Account progression

Guest, Viewer, Creator, Verified, and Select are working concepts. Their exact eligibility, privileges, review process, and upgrade requirements remain OPEN.

## Product quality

- Arabic and English are first-class languages.
- RTL and LTR are structural, not cosmetic.
- Discovery should reward relevance, context, diversity, and quality—not raw engagement alone.
- Respectful community interaction and creator safety are core product requirements.
- Rights, attribution, and provenance must be represented without assuming that an upload proves ownership.
