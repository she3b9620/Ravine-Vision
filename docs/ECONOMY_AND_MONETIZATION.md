# Economy and Monetization

Economic design is a first-class platform domain. It must account for sustainable revenue for RAVINE, fair earning opportunities for creators, and appropriate value-sharing opportunities for participating users.

## Opportunity areas to evaluate

- Creator memberships and audience subscriptions.
- Tips, gifts, and direct patronage.
- Paid works, rentals, tickets, and premium experiences.
- Licensing, syndication, and rights-managed distribution.
- Creative services and commissions marketplace.
- Sponsorships and clearly disclosed partnerships.
- Affiliate and referral programs with anti-abuse controls.
- Grants, challenges, awards, and a future RAVINE Fund.
- RAVINE Originals and commissioned productions.
- Educational products, events, and community-supported projects.
- Optional platform plans and professional tools.

These are areas for research and architecture, not a declaration that every model will launch.

## Required foundations

- A transparent, auditable ledger for all balances and movements.
- Explicit records of gross amount, fees, taxes where applicable, refunds, chargebacks, and net distributions.
- Clear rights, consent, attribution, and licensing terms.
- Eligibility and payout status separated from account popularity.
- Fraud, collusion, manipulation, and abuse detection.
- User-visible statements and dispute workflows.
- Country, currency, sanctions, tax, and payment-provider constraints.
- Separation between platform revenue, creator earnings, and participant rewards.

## Approval boundary

No fee rate, commission, revenue share, payout threshold, eligibility rule, payment provider, or launch commitment is approved by this document. Those remain OPEN until explicitly decided.
